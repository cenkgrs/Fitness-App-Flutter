Delete your THRIVE+ account

THRIVE+ · SilverOak Technologies

You can permanently delete your THRIVE+ account and all associated data directly from within the app. No separate request or waiting period is needed — deletion is immediate.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the THRIVE+ app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion in the dialog that appears.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your profile (name, age, height, weight, fitness level, goals)
  • Your workout programs and workout history (sessions, sets, reps, weights)
  • Your nutrition logs and meal entries
  • Your weight and body-measurement history
  • Your app settings

This action is immediate and irreversible — there is no recovery period, and no data is retained after deletion.

Can't access the app?

If you're unable to sign in to request deletion through the app, email support@silveroaktech.app from the email address associated with your account and we will delete it manually.
